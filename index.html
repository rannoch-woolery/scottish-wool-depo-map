    Reading this map. Gap score ranks each parish on sheep count and road distance to its nearest depot (percentile rank on each, multiplied) — high on both is what lights up. 230 of 891 parishes have sheep counts withheld by the Scottish Government for disclosure control (fewer than five holdings, or two holdings ≥85% of the total) — shown grey and excluded from scoring. Depot list covers British Wool's Scotland & near-border network (47 of 147 UK locations).

    Shetland is excluded. Most Shetland wool is sold through Jamieson & Smith, an independent Shetland-based broker that buys direct from 700+ crofters — a 2009 trading standards survey found they purchase over 80% of the islands' clip. That's a separate system from the British Wool depot network this map scores, so including Shetland here would compare it against an alternative it largely doesn't use. All 23 Shetland parishes are excluded from the gap score and its percentile ranking (not just hidden after the fact).

    How distances are measured. Each parish is represented by the geometric centroid of its official boundary (area-weighted centre of mass, computed from the full-detail polygon before any map simplification) — not the population-weighted centre of its farms, and not its furthest point. For an oddly-shaped or very large parish the centroid may sit some distance from where most of its sheep actually are. Road distance is then routed from that centroid to the nearest depot by OSRM's driving profile (which includes ferry crossings), not a straight line.

    Data sources

    1. Sheep counts & holdings by parish: Scottish Government, June Agricultural Census 2023, parish-level ad hoc data (published 19 Dec 2024 — the most recent year with parish-level detail; 2024–25 census results exist only at county/region level).
    2. Parish boundaries: Scottish Government, Agricultural Parishes shapefile (2016), Open Government Licence v3.0.
    3. Depot & drop-off locations: British Wool depot network, pulled Aug 2026.
    4. Road distances: OSRM public routing engine, driving profile.
    5. Shetland wool marketing: Jamieson & Smith, About Us.
